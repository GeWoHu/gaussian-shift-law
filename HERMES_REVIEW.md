## 1. The shift is already explained
The classical invariant ring of binary sextics has Hilbert series

    Σ g₆(ℓ)t^ℓ = (1 + t¹⁵) / ((1−t²)(1−t⁴)(1−t⁶)(1−t¹⁰)).

Because the denominator contains only even powers, g₆(ℓ) = g₆(ℓ−15) for odd
ℓ ≥ 15 follows immediately. The same generating function follows directly from
Appendix A.2(1)–(2) of Krattenthaler–Kronholm–Marsh (arXiv:2510.14124), which
the manuscript already cites. The reviewer independently checked the subtraction
by exact polynomial cross-multiplication: zero residual.

## 2. The "unknown mechanism" is classical
The invariant ring is generated by even invariants of degrees 2, 4, 6, 10 plus
an odd generator E of degree 15 with a single relation E² = P(even generators).
Multiplication by E identifies the even degree-(ℓ−15) component with the odd
degree-ℓ component. "No representation-theoretic explanation is known" was not
defensible. A direct partition bijection remains an interesting open question.
